# Acme Operations Pte Ltd — HR & Leave Policy Handbook

## 1. Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year (pro-rated in the first year).
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried forward to the next calendar year and must be used by March 31.
- Leave requests of 3 days or fewer require manager notice 3 working days in advance; 4+ days require 2 weeks notice.

## 2. Medical & Hospitalization Leave
- Employees are entitled to **14 days of paid outpatient sick leave** and **60 days of paid hospitalization leave** (inclusive of outpatient sick leave) per year with a valid medical certificate (MC).

## 3. Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
- Core office days are **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**.
- Monday and Friday are flexible work-from-home days.
- Up to 10 additional remote workdays per year may be requested with manager approval.

## 4. Expense & Reimbursement Policy
- Business expenses under **SGD 250** with itemized receipts are auto-reconciled internally.
- Outbound reimbursements and expenses above SGD 250 require Finance Controller approval prior to payout.
- Meals during client meetings are capped at SGD 60 per person.

## 5. Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 16 weeks (eligible Singapore citizens) or 12 weeks statutory.
- Paternity leave: 4 weeks paid paternity leave.
